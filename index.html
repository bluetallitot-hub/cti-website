Jewish life in Peabody since 1922

Welcome to Congregation Tifereth Israel

For more than a century, Congregation Tifereth Israel has been a home for Jewish life in Peabody, Massachusetts.

Founded by Sephardic Jewish families, our congregation continues to cherish the traditions, melodies, and customs passed down through generations. Over the years, our community has grown to embrace people from many different Jewish backgrounds, each adding something to our shared story.

We may be a small congregation, but our history is rich, our traditions are enduring, and our doors are open.

Whether you are joining us for services, exploring your Jewish heritage, or simply looking for a place to belong, we invite you to become part of our community.
