IN SEARCH OF A HOME: Small synagogue remembers a big part of history

The surviving primary clipping identifies this as a Salem Evening News story from October 31, 1992, credited to “A News staff report.” It discusses Congregation Tifereth Israel, Ladino, the congregation’s Sephardic history, and the November 4, 1992 Sephardic quincentennial commemoration.

The Jewish Historical Society of the North Shore catalog independently lists two videotapes of the CTI/North Shore Jewish Historical Society quincentennial event.
