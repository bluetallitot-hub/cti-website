Original source: The Jewish Advocate, April 14–20, 1995, page 12. Archival scan I-581-01-15-035.

Peabody's Jewish communities

This feature describes the history of Jewish life in Peabody, including both Ashkenazi and Sephardic congregations. It traces Congregation Tifereth Israel to families who came from Constantinople and established a congregation in 1922, and describes the congregation's distinctive Sephardic religious and cultural traditions.

Joseph Gibely, identified as a major focal point of Jewish activity on the North Shore, speaks about the congregation's history, Sephardic cooking, and its relationship with other Jewish communities.
