Secretary

Joanne Pressman

Congregation Tifereth Israel

Joanne Pressman serves as Secretary of Congregation Tifereth Israel for the 2026 board cycle, but her connection to CTI reaches far beyond a single term of office.

Joanne is the granddaughter of two of the congregation's original founders. Over the years she has served as a historian, cantorial soloist, and one of the congregation's most important transmitters of its Sephardic musical and liturgical traditions.

Her work has helped preserve melodies, customs, and communal memory that originated with the Turkish Sephardic families who established CTI in 1922. That role has made her an important bridge between the founding generation and the congregation today.

A 2019 Jewish Journal feature on CTI's Sephardic traditions photographed Joanne at or near the congregation's ark with Elliot Hershoff and Doris Hershoff.
