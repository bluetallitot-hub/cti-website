75th Anniversary

May 18, 1997

CTI anniversary archive

The Ladies Auxiliary sponsored Congregation Tifereth Israel’s 75th Anniversary Dinner on May 18, 1997. The surviving invitation and program preserve CTI’s own anniversary record.

The program chronology records the February 15, 1922 charter and preserves information about charter members, presidents, religious leaders, and congregational milestones.
