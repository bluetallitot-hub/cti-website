Rabbi Phillip Blue

Educator, lifelong student of Torah, and community leader.

Teaching, learning, and community

Rabbi Phillip Blue is an educator, lifelong student of Torah, and community leader whose work bridges traditional Jewish learning with contemporary Jewish life. A lifelong resident of the North Shore, he brings to Congregation Tifereth Israel a deep love of Jewish learning, teaching, prayer, and tradition.

His approach emphasizes close reading of Jewish texts, classical commentary, historical context, and the enduring relevance of Torah to modern life. He values an approachable rabbinate and respects the individual Jewish journeys of the people he serves.

At CTI, Rabbi Blue supports the congregation's longstanding customs while continuing to learn and preserve its distinctive Sephardic traditions and minhagim.
