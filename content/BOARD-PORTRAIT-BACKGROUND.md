## Rules for future board portraits

1. **Always retrieve the approved background asset** from the path above once it is uploaded. Never substitute an invented synagogue, stained glass, a Star of David, a wood-paneled room, or another background.
2. Keep the background's visible landmarks: blurred green plant and pale planter on the left, cool cream/gray wall at center, framed art and warm glowing light on the right.
3. Preserve the board member's recognizable facial features and identity when compositing. Match the head-and-shoulders crop, lighting, and overall visual treatment to the existing board portraits.
4. Never overwrite Jerry's original portrait or other originals. Save new portraits as distinct assets.
5. For a future CTI-owned repository, retain this relative asset path unchanged.
