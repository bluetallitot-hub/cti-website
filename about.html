About CTI

A welcoming Jewish community with a distinctive Sephardic heritage.

Our SynagogueSephardic HeritageMembershipVisit Us

A community shaped by tradition

Formally founded on February 15, 1922 by Sephardic Jewish families, many from Constantinople and the Ladino-speaking Jewish world, CTI grew from an immigrant community that began taking root on the North Shore before World War I. It has remained a home for prayer, learning, friendship, and Jewish life in Peabody.

Over the generations, members from many Jewish backgrounds have become part of the congregation while CTI has continued to preserve Sephardic customs, melodies, and communal memory.
