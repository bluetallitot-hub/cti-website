About CTI

Who We Are

Congregation Tifereth Israel is a small, welcoming Jewish community in Peabody with more than a century of history and a distinctive Sephardic heritage—one of the relatively few Sephardic congregations in New England.

A community shaped by tradition

Formally founded on February 15, 1922 by Sephardic Jewish families, many from Constantinople and the Ladino-speaking Jewish world, CTI grew from an immigrant community that began taking root on the North Shore before World War I. It has remained a home for prayer, learning, friendship, and Jewish life in Peabody.

Over the generations, members from many Jewish backgrounds have become part of the congregation while CTI has continued to preserve Sephardic customs, melodies, and communal memory.

Our Story

More than a century in Peabody

From its beginnings in private homes and rented space to its permanent home on Pierpont Street, CTI's story reflects both continuity and change.

1922

Sephardic families establish Congregation Tifereth Israel in Peabody.

1955

The congregation acquires its permanent home at 8 Pierpont Street.

2022

CTI celebrates one hundred years of Jewish life and community.
