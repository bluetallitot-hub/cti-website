Rabbi's Corner

Torah, learning, conversation, and connection with Rabbi Blue.

Rabbi Phillip Blue is an educator, lifelong student of Torah, and community leader whose work bridges traditional Jewish learning with contemporary Jewish life. A lifelong resident of the North Shore, he brings to Congregation Tifereth Israel a deep love of Jewish learning, teaching, prayer, and tradition.

Education and the Path to the Rabbinate

Rabbi Blue is a graduate of the University of Massachusetts Amherst, where he earned degrees in Judaic Studies and Computer Science along with a minor in Hebrew. His academic background reflects two interests that have continued throughout his life: careful study of Jewish texts and a practical, analytical approach to understanding complex ideas.

Rabbi Blue pursued rabbinic ordination later in life, fulfilling a longstanding aspiration inspired in part by the example of others who entered the rabbinate after established careers. He received semikhah from the American Board of Rabbis in 2025. His rabbinic studies included work under Rabbi Mordechai Yitzchok Friedman, and he was also mentored by Rabbi Emeritus Richard Perlman of Temple Ner Tamid in Peabody.

A Life of Jewish Learning

For more than two decades, Daf Yomi has been a central part of Rabbi Blue’s Jewish learning. He has completed two full cycles of the Talmud and continues through a third. That long engagement with Talmud shapes both his teaching and his approach to Jewish life: patient attention to the text, respect for multiple voices, and a willingness to wrestle seriously with difficult questions.

Over the years, Rabbi Blue has taught in Hebrew schools, Jewish day schools, summer camps, adult-learning settings, and ongoing Torah classes. His teaching ranges across Torah, Talmud, halakhah, classical commentary, and Jewish history. He particularly values text-based learning in which participants can see how an idea develops from the biblical text through the classical commentators and into later Jewish thought.

Approach to Torah and Community

Rabbi Blue’s approach to Jewish learning emphasizes close reading, classical commentaries, historical context, and the enduring relevance of Jewish tradition to modern life. He believes that serious Jewish learning can be intellectually rigorous without becoming inaccessible, and that a rabbi should help people encounter Jewish texts directly rather than simply presenting conclusions.

He also believes that a rabbi should be approachable and available. Whether through a class, a sermon, a conversation after services, or a private question, his goal is to help people find meaning in Torah, mitzvot, prayer, and Jewish tradition while respecting each person’s individual Jewish journey.

Jewish Continuity and Personal Story

The son of an Israeli father and the grandson of Holocaust survivors, Rabbi Blue’s commitment to Jewish continuity is deeply personal. For him, Jewish life is not only an intellectual tradition but also a living inheritance: something received from earlier generations, practiced in the present, and passed on with care.

That sense of continuity informs his interest in the ways communities preserve their own customs, melodies, stories, and local traditions. He sees those details not as peripheral to Jewish life, but as part of what makes a congregation feel like a particular Jewish home.

Congregation Tifereth Israel

At Congregation Tifereth Israel, Rabbi Blue is committed to supporting the congregation’s longstanding customs while continuing to learn its distinctive Sephardic traditions and minhagim. He values CTI’s dedicated lay leadership and the generations of members who have preserved the congregation’s melodies, practices, and communal memory.

His work at CTI brings together many of the themes that have shaped his Jewish life: prayer, teaching, close study of Jewish texts, respect for inherited tradition, and the responsibility to make Jewish life meaningful and accessible to the next generation.

Beyond his work in the congregation, Rabbi Blue also makes custom tallitot, bringing together Jewish tradition, personal expression, and the craftsmanship of ritual garments. His work can be found at Blue Designs Judaica.

Family

Rabbi Blue and his wife, Miriam, who serves as Director of the Hillel Council of New England, have built a home centered on Jewish learning, community, and family. Together they are raising their two children with a deep commitment to Jewish education and tradition.

Whether teaching Torah, leading prayer, delivering a D’var Torah, or simply sitting in conversation, Rabbi Blue seeks to make Jewish texts accessible, thoughtful, and meaningful while fostering a sense of connection, shared purpose, and belonging within the communities he serves.
