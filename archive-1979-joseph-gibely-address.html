Joseph Gibely address on CTI and Sephardic history

March 1, 1979

Primary CTI document

This twelve-page address by CTI president Joseph Gibely is a primary source for the congregation’s own account of its history. It records the February 15, 1922 founding, Sephardic migration history, Ladino vocabulary, customs, and traditions remembered within the congregation.

Primary source: Jewish Community of Peabody (Mass.) Records I-581, Box 1, Folder 15, images 012–023, Wyner Family Jewish Heritage Center.
