A congregation's Sephardic roots

In this 2001 feature, Gary Band traces the journey of Congregation Tifereth Israel's founding families from Constantinople to the North Shore, and describes the congregation's history and Sephardic traditions.

The 1914 family photograph

The article identifies Morris Gibely as one of the original seven charter members of Tifereth Israel. The photograph shows Morris (center) in 1914 with his wife Sarah, his brother David, and children Rose, Robert, and Stella. The caption notes that the youngest child, Joseph, was born six years later.

The article also includes a photograph of the congregation's building in Peabody.
